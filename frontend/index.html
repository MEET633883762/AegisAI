HEADERS ONLY

A SMALL, CONTROLLED SCOPE

Built to demonstrate.
Clear about its limits.

The public experience serves reviewed sample files. It does not execute simulations or contact websites.

  • — No public simulation runs, uploads, or user-submitted targets.
  • — No public LLM calls, accounts, billing, or customer data store.
  • — The local header check covers selected response headers and status only.

Synthetic demonstrations do not establish real-world security effectiveness or certify a website as secure.
